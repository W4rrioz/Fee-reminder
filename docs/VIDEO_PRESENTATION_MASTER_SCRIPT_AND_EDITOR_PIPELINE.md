## 4. How the Automated Editing Pipeline Works

You don't need Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or After Effects. Follow these 3 simple steps:

```
[Record Video Files] 
       │
       ▼
 Drop into `videos/raw/` 
 (e.g. `facecam.mp4` and `screen_demo.mp4`, or one combined `recording.mp4`)
       │
       ▼
 Run Automated Pipeline Script:
 `python scripts/stitch_presentation_video.py`
       │
       ▼
 [Final 1080p Broadcast MP4 with Motion Graphics & Audio Leveling Generated]
```

### Script Execution Summary:
- **Audio Clean:** Automatically normalizes voice volume to -14 LUFS (broadcast standard) and reduces ambient hum.
- **Auto-Sync:** Aligns timestamps based on the script cues above.
- **Motion Overlays:** Layers the 60+ reel motion graphic elements (stamps, tracing beams, text highlights) directly at the cue points.
- **Output:** Saves the finalized video to `EduManage_Bharat_3Min_Hackathon_Pitch.mp4` in full 1080p.
